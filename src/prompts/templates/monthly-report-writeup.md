2. **Read the numbers correctly.**
   - In `search.vsPreviousMonth` and `search.vsLastYear`, the `change` for `clicks` and `impressions` is a percent. For `ctr` it is percentage points, and for `position` it is places, where a negative number is an improvement (lower is better). The same applies to `positionDelta` in `queryMovers` and `pageMovers`; `clicksDelta` is a plain click count.
   - Each of `search.vsPreviousMonth` and `search.vsLastYear` has a `value` (this month), a `change`, and a `previous` object with the earlier period's `clicks`, `impressions`, `ctr` and `position`. Quote the percent next to the absolute numbers from `value` and `previous`, rounded sensibly. The branded and non-branded blocks give both `current` and `previous` too.
   - If `vsLastYear` (or `vsPreviousMonth`) is `null`, or its `previous` is `null` and its changes are all `null`, the earlier period has no data (for example the Search Console property is new), so leave that comparison out and don't quote any change from it.
3. **Write the report** with exactly these sections:
   - **Summary.** Three sentences: how organic search did overall, the main reason, and the one thing to watch.
   - **What went up, and why.** Use `search.vsPreviousMonth`, `search.vsLastYear`, `organicTraffic`, `queryMovers`, `pageMovers` and `topLandingPages`. Explain the likely reason only when the data supports it (for example a page or query that gained). If the data doesn't say why, say it isn't clear from the data. Don't invent causes.
   - **What went down, and why.** Same approach. Don't hide declines.
   - **Branded vs non-branded.** Use `search.branded`, `search.nonBranded` and `search.brandedShareOfClicks`. Branded searches are people who already know the business by name; non-branded are people who found it without knowing it, so non-branded is the better sign of SEO working. Report each separately and read the note in the split about what it leaves out. Sanity-check the split: if a top non-branded query in `queryMovers` or `topQueries` is clearly a spelling or variant of the business name, say the branded share is probably understated and suggest the `review-brand-terms` prompt. If `search.error` is set, or the split itself reports an error, or the client has no `brandTerms`, say the split is unavailable or unreliable and why, and don't estimate it.
   - **Issues fixed since the last audit.** Use `audit` (its diff of resolved, new and persistent findings and any score changes). If `audit` is `null`, has an `error`, or has no earlier audit to compare against, write "No audit comparison is available for this month" and move on. Don't mention fixes that aren't in the diff. Findings marked unverified were not re-checked, so don't count them as fixed.
   - **Top 3 opportunities.** From `opportunities` (striking-distance queries, low-CTR top results and cannibalized queries), pick the three with the most estimated extra clicks. Treat cannibalization with care: if many of the client's other pages show the same tiny impression count and zero clicks for a query, that is usually site links under the home page's result, not real competition between pages, so say so rather than telling the client to merge pages. For each give the query or page, where it ranks now and the specific action. If `opportunities` has an `error`, say so instead.
   - **Next month's priorities.** Three to five concrete actions drawn from the above.
